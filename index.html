How to read a binding
  • Model name is on the heel piece (e.g. "Colt 7", "Icon 10", "SPX 10", "Evo 9", "Race 10").
  • The small window with numbers is the release setting (DIN). The number after the model name is roughly the top setting.
  • A tech sets it for M's weight, height, age and boot. Do not set it yourself.
  • Atomic Colt 7 fits boots with a sole length up to 266 mm only. Check her boot.

Rules for M (U12)

When the club and Alpine Ontario differ, the club rule wins.

One helmet for both events

Buy a hard-ear helmet with an FIS sticker and a chin guard that comes off. Slalom needs the chin guard. GS does not allow it.

Race suit

Allowed in U12, but the club says 10-year-olds cannot wear one at the local hill races. M counts as 10 all season. Skip it this year unless it is very cheap.

Handing down to Hailey

  • Helmet: OK to hand down if it was never in a hard crash and is under 5 years old.
  • Skis and bindings: sized by weight. Hailey fits them when she reaches about 80 lbs.
  • Keep a note of each binding's model and year. Shops ask.
  • Guards and suits: buy a size up so they last two kids.
